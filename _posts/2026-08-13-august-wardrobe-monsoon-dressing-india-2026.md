August has a particular personality in the Indian wardrobe calendar. The monsoon, which arrived in June with dramatic intent and spent July making its presence felt in the most inconvenient ways possible, has by August settled into a kind of comfortable permanence. It is no longer a dramatic event. It is simply the condition of life: the morning that begins clear and clouds over by noon, the afternoon shower that was not forecast, the evening that turns cool and salt-aired and unexpectedly beautiful.

Dressing for August requires a different kind of intelligence from dressing for any other month. The problem is not the rain itself, which the right wardrobe can manage. The problem is the unpredictability: the day that begins like summer and ends like October, the outdoor lunch that becomes an indoor one halfway through, the commute that encounters weather in three distinct moods between home and destination. The August wardrobe is, more than any other, the wardrobe that has to do multiple things simultaneously. And the most interesting clothes are always the ones that rise to that brief without appearing to try.

**The Larger Point**

The August wardrobe, at its most intelligent, is not about dressing despite the weather. It is about dressing with it. The month that most wardrobe calendars treat as an unfortunate interruption to the summer and an awkward preamble to the festive season is, with the right pieces and the right attention, one of the more interesting dressing challenges the Indian year produces.

The rain is not going anywhere for a while. The wardrobe might as well rise to meet it.
