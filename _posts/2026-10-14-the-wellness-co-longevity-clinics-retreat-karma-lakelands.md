There is a quiet shift under way in how affluent India thinks about health. The old model waited for something to break and then paid handsomely to fix it. The new one, favoured by a generation of founders, executives and athletes, starts earlier and asks a different question: not how long can I live, but how well. The **[Wellness Co. ](https://www.thewellnessco.in/)**has built its business on that question.

Co-founded by Rishabh Jain and his brother, the company now operates integrative wellness and longevity clinics across eight Indian cities, from Defence Colony and Golf Course Road to Marine Drive, Bandra, Banjara Hills, Chennai’s TTK Road, Ahmedabad, Ludhiana and three addresses in Bengaluru. This season, it is adding something larger: a retreat at Karma Lakelands, built to take the same philosophy and give it room to breathe.



![](/assets/images/posts/screenshot-2026-10-14-at-4.51.55 pm.png)



**A Founding Idea**

Jain puts the ambition plainly. “We set out with a clear yet ambitious vision: to transform the way people approach health by moving from treating illness to prioritising prevention, and from simply extending lifespan to improving quality of life.” The company’s thesis is that true wellness begins at the cellular level, where advanced science, personalised diagnostics, nutrition, recovery technologies and mindful living can address underlying causes rather than managing symptoms.

His measure of success is refreshingly unquantified. “Our success isn’t measured by how many therapies we offer,” he says, “but by how meaningfully we improve people’s lives.” The longer aspiration is a national one: to make India a global destination for science-led wellness.

**Inside the Clinics**

The clinic experience is built around three organising principles the company returns to again and again: oxygenation, hydration and circulation. The therapy menu follows. Whole Body Cryotherapy for recovery, IV drip therapy with more than twenty formulations, hyperbaric oxygen therapy, red light therapy, far infrared sauna, EMS training, advanced genetic screening and Hydrafacial Syndeo sit alongside wellness diagnostics, slimming and weight management, pain and athletic recovery, and mind wellness and relaxation. The company says the therapies it offers are USFDA, CE or MFDS approved.

The audience, by the brand’s own description, is high achievers, men and women at the top of the success ladder who carry real strain on both mind and body, and who want a tailored plan rather than a generic one.



![](/assets/images/posts/screenshot-2026-10-14-at-4.52.15 pm.png)



**The Retreat at Karma Lakelands**

If the clinics are the daily practice, the retreat is the deep dive. Set within Karma Lakelands on NH-8 in Sector 80, Gurugram, **[The Wellness Co. Retreat ](https://www.thewellnessco.in/retreats)**is positioned as a sanctuary where science meets nature: a year-round ecosystem that blends precision diagnostics, recovery therapies, nutrition, Ayurveda and mindfulness. The retreat is currently in a pre-launch phase, with a priority list and limited-time Founding Stays for its earliest guests.

The programme is structured like a medical journey rather than a holiday. It begins before arrival with a concierge intake and goal-mapping, moves to arrival diagnostics that establish a personal baseline, and then settles into a daily protocol of therapies, movement, mindfulness and recovery. A phase of “deep work” targets root causes of stress, fatigue and metabolic imbalance, and each stay ends with a re-entry plan, a personalised at-home protocol with continued support through the clinic network.

The facilities map to that thinking. A Health Optimization Lab houses red light therapy, HBOT, cryotherapy, IV drips, Normatec compression, far infrared sauna and PEMF. A Strength and Mobility Zone covers EMS workouts, Pilates and mobility work. Ayurveda and naturopathy sit alongside contrast therapy with cold plunges, steam and sauna. Meditation, breathwork and sound healing complete the picture, and the kitchen’s “culinary precision” is built around each guest’s goals and biomarkers.

Stays come in four lengths: three nights (The Reset), five (The Recalibration), seven (The Transformation) and fourteen (The Renewal).



![](/assets/images/posts/screenshot-2026-10-14-at-4.52.21 pm.png)



**The Sleep Chapter**

Of the retreat’s six health pillars, one speaks directly to a problem many of its guests will arrive with. Sleep Architecture and Nervous System Regulation is built around recalibrating sleep cycles and calming the nervous system for deeper rest, stress resilience and steadier daily energy. It is a pillar that makes intuitive sense for the stated audience: people whose days run hot and whose nights rarely recover.

The three-night Reset is the most direct entry point. It is designed to calm an overworked nervous system, improve sleep quality and reduce underlying inflammation through restorative therapies, guided mindfulness, recovery technologies and personalised consultations. Longer stays then build on that baseline, with the retreat’s follow-through model aiming to carry improved sleep and energy home, rather than leaving them at check-out.

Several client testimonials on the company’s site cite better sleep after therapies such as cryotherapy and red light sessions, though as with any wellness treatment, individual experiences vary and a physician’s guidance is worth seeking for persistent sleep problems.

**The Continuity Argument**

What may ultimately distinguish The Wellness Co. is the loop it is trying to close. Many luxury retreats leave guests feeling wonderful for a week and unchanged by the month’s end. Here, the retreat plan transitions into clinic-supported follow-through, with coaching, therapy sessions and accountability at the brand’s flagship clinics, so that biomarkers, habits and results keep compounding long after the stay.

It is a quietly ambitious proposition, and one that fits the founders’ stated philosophy. If preventive healthcare is going to become part of everyday Indian life, as Jain hopes, it will need infrastructure that lives in cities and in sanctuaries, and a method that links the two.
