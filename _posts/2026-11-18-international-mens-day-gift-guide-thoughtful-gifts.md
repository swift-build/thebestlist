**Gifting Etiquette, Briefly**

* Personalise where you can. A handwritten note changes everything.
* Choose quality over quantity. One considered gift beats three forgettable ones.
* Think about use. The best gifts for men are things he will actually reach for.
* Mind the size and fit before you buy clothing, and keep the receipt.
* Don’t overthink the price. Attention is the real luxury.

**The Finish**
