Some hotels are a place to sleep. Others are the reason you booked the trip.

India’s heritage stays belong firmly to the second group. They are buildings that were meant to impress, with domes, courtyards, jharokhas and ballrooms made for an audience of one court or one family. Many were saved from decline by restoration and given a second life as hotels, with the architecture left to do most of the talking.

This is a shortlist of the ones worth checking into, chosen because the building and the hospitality are equally compelling.
