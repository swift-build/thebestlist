Some arrivals have been a long time coming. For years, Indian brides, wedding guests and red-carpet regulars have turned to Elie Saab for the kind of dressing that does not need an explanation: the sweep of tulle, the precision of embroidery, the quiet confidence of a gown designed for an occasion. This October, the Beirut-born house finally gives that relationship an address. Elie Saab has opened its first boutique in India, at DLF Emporio in New Delhi.



![](/assets/images/posts/screenshot-2026-10-15-at-6.12.09 pm.png)



**A New Chapter**

The opening, in the House’s own words, marks a new chapter in its longstanding relationship with its Indian clientele, and the history is real. Elie Saab has been embraced in India for weddings, celebrations and special occasions over the years, a natural fit for a country that treats dressing up as a serious art form. The relationship has also extended beyond fashion: the House previously established a presence in Delhi through real-estate collaborations, further tying its name to the city and its clientele before a single garment hung in a Delhi boutique.

**Inside the Space**

Spanning 161 square metres, the boutique brings together Elie Saab ready-to-wear, perfumes and accessories, alongside the House’s kidswear collection. It is a considered edit of the brand’s world, rather than only its most theatrical moments, designed to serve a client who might arrive for an evening dress and leave with a fragrance and something for her daughter.

The interiors draw inspiration from the Elie Saab Maison collection, with bespoke furnishings and wallpaper set against white marble and bronzed brass. An illuminated Elie Saab monogram forms a defining architectural element of the space, a quiet but unmistakable signature. The overall effect, as the House describes it, is a refined dialogue of materials, light and craftsmanship.

**The Private Salon**

For clients who prefer to shop without an audience, a private salon offers a more intimate setting for personalised appointments and one-to-one service. In a market where the most memorable fashion decisions often involve family, planners and a trusted opinion or two, it is a thoughtful addition, and one that signals how the House expects to be used here: not as a place to browse, but as a destination to book.

**A Global Map, Now with Delhi**

The Delhi boutique joins an international footprint that already spans Paris, Beirut, London, New York, Milan, Monaco, Dubai, Qatar, Abu Dhabi and Riyadh. Adding New Delhi to that list is as much a statement of intent as a retail milestone. India’s luxury consumer is increasingly well travelled, well informed and used to being served by the world’s great houses in person, and a boutique at DLF Emporio, one of the capital’s established luxury addresses, puts Elie Saab in that conversation.

**The Wider Picture**

For the House, the opening brings its collections and its world of craftsmanship closer to its Indian clients. For the clients, it removes a layer of distance: fittings, appointments and discoveries that once required a flight to Dubai or London can now happen in Delhi. And in a season dominated by festive dressing and a wedding calendar that shows no sign of slowing, the timing could hardly be more apt.

Elie Saab built its reputation on the idea that a dress can mark a moment. Delhi, a city that marks a great many of them, now has a place to find it.
