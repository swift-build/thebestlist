There is a particular kind of light that arrives with Ganpati... brass diyas catching the last of the September sun, marigolds stacked in steel thalis, the smell of modak steam drifting through open windows. It is, without question, one of the most photogenic weeks on the Indian calendar. And this year, the women (and men) who move through it best aren't reaching for the loudest outfit in the closet. They're reaching for the most *considered* one.

Call it the Ganpati Edit: a wardrobe moment that sits somewhere between heirloom and editorial, where a nine-yard drape can share a frame with a structured blouse, and where the accessories often do more talking than the outfit itself.
