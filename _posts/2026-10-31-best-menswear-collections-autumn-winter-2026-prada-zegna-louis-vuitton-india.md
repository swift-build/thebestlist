Every menswear season asks the same question: what do men actually want to wear? For autumn/winter 2026, the answer is clothes with history, tailoring with ease, and layers built to be lived in. Designers are moving away from stiffness and spectacle and towards pieces that feel personal, considered and made to last.

As the season settles into wardrobes, here are the collections worth coveting, from global houses to the Indian labels shaping how the modern man dresses.

The U.S. Polo Assn. Men's AW '26 collection marks the fifth season of its celebrated collaboration with HH Padmanabh Singh (Pacho), the brand's Global Brand Ambassador, setting the tone with an autumnal palette, alongside transitional and winter-ready knitwear and layering. Varsity influences return through contemporary interpretations of classic American codes, while indigo emerges as a defining seasonal story across polo shirts, sweatshirts, jackets and denim.
