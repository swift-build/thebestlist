Every menswear season asks the same question: what do men actually want to wear? For autumn/winter 2026, the answer is clothes with history, tailoring with ease, and layers built to be lived in. Designers are moving away from stiffness and spectacle and towards pieces that feel personal, considered and made to last.

As the season settles into wardrobes, here are the collections worth coveting, from global houses to the Indian labels shaping how the modern man dresses.

**Five Trends Running Through the Season**

* **70s tailoring returns**. Softer shoulders, wider lapels and relaxed trousers replace rigid suits.
* **Preppy, reworked**. Blazers over knits and denim, rugby shirts and cable sweaters, styled in a more lived-in way.
* **Designed for real life**. Suits met puffers, technical fabrics hid inside classic coats, and layers moved easily between work and weekend.
* **Proportion play**. Longer sleeves, fuller trousers and cropped jackets did the work that loud prints used to do. Brown tones appeared across many of the strongest looks.

The throughline this winter is restraint with personality. The most coveted collections do not shout. They offer coats that feel inherited, tailoring that moves, and details that reward a second look, and they ask men to dress with intent.
