The most interesting groomswear right now isn't coming from the names everyone already knows. It's coming from a quieter tier of designers, ones building genuinely considered pieces without needing the noise of a big label to justify the price tag. Here are five pieces worth knowing about.



![](/assets/images/posts/laromanikurta.png)



**[1. Green Digital Printed Zari Embroidered Kurta Set, Laromani](https://laromaniofficial.com/products/green-digital-printed-zari-embroidered-kurta-set-for-men-lr-1660-green)**

When occasion wear calls for effortless sophistication, this Green Digital Printed Zari Embroidered Kurta Set by Laromani earns a well-deserved spot on our monthly wardrobe wishlist. Crafted in luxurious Chinon silk, the kurta features intricate zari embroidery layered over refined digital prints, striking the perfect balance between timeless craftsmanship and contemporary design. Paired with classic cotton silk trousers, the ensemble offers comfort without compromising on elegance. The rich green hue makes it a versatile pick for festive celebrations, wedding functions, and evening soirées, while the mandarin collar and clean silhouette lend a polished finish. It's the kind of investment piece that feels equally appropriate for a mehendi celebration, a family wedding, or a festive dinner, making it one of the smartest additions to a modern man's ethnic wardrobe this season.



![](/assets/images/posts/untitledsession5848_d36ebcb7-a598-4ab2-b83c-38c0bcf158d2.webp)



**2. [PhOOLPANKH HALDI JODHPURI SET from Kalpraag](https://kalpraag.com/products/poolpankh-haldi-jodhpuri-set?_pos=1&_psq=PhOOLPANKH+HALDI+JODHPURI+SET&_psid=69af0cbb2&_ss=e)**

This Jodhpuri set features a structured, tailored jacket with intricate embroidery and detailed floral-inspired motifs, giving it a refined and artistic appeal. It is styled over a shirt with subtle textural detailing, paired with classic trousers. The ensemble blends traditional craftsmanship with a modern silhouette, making it ideal for festive or formal occasions.



![](/assets/images/posts/image3_6.webp)



**3. [Dahre Anjum Sherwani Set from Tasva](https://www.torani.in/products/rishab-rikhiram-sharma-in-our-dahre-anjum-sherwani-set?_pos=1&_sid=2d38cf1f7&_ss=r)**

Crafted in luxurious Jeni silk and muslin silk, the Dahre Anjum Sherwani Set is adorned with intricate dori and dabka embroidery that celebrates India's rich artisanal heritage. Complete with a sherwani, kurta, and churidaar, the ensemble is a study in timeless sophistication.



![](/assets/images/posts/file_a2ed69d6-5465-484a-a913-c49ac1dae104.webp)



[**4.** Abstract Sequin Embellished Kurta from Kasbah](https://kasbahmen.com/products/abstract-sequin-embellished-kurta?_pos=1&_psq=Abstract+Sequin+Embellished+Kurta&_psid=3fec79c1f&_ss=e)

Crafted in fluid georgette, this abstract sequin-embellished kurta reimagines festive dressing through a contemporary lens. The all-over shimmer lends depth and movement, creating a look that's effortlessly refined

**5. [Mauve Handwoven Silk Embroidered Sherwani Set from Bhasin Brothers](https://bhasinbrothers.com/products/mauve-handwoven-silk-embroidered-sherwani-set)**

Draped in the quiet opulence of handwoven silk, this mauve sherwani set is a masterclass in understated grandeur. The rich mauve hue, a timeless blend of dusty rose and soft violet, catches light with a natural silk sheen that no synthetic fabric can replicate. Every inch of this ensemble speaks of artisanal precision: intricate thread embroidery cascades across the chest, cuffs, and hemline in traditional motifs that honour centuries of Indian craftsmanship.

**The Common Thread**

None of these five pieces shout. Each one relies on fabric, cut, and restraint to make its case, a reflection of where groomswear is actually heading. The modern groom isn't looking to compete with the bride's outfit anymore. He's looking for pieces considered enough to not need to.
