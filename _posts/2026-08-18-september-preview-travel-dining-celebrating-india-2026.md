There is a particular quality to September in India that the months on either side of it do not quite possess. August is still the monsoon's territory, insistent and beautiful and occasionally exhausting. October belongs to the festive season in full throat, loud and joyful and slightly overwhelming. September sits between those two states with a quality of its own: the rains beginning to ease, the light returning to something cleaner and more golden, the air carrying the particular freshness of a landscape that has been thoroughly washed and is now drying in the sun.

It is the month that rewards the organised. The hotel rooms worth having have been booked. The reservations at the restaurants worth attending are confirmed. The wardrobe for the celebrations beginning at the end of the month is already assembled. September, for the Indian traveller and diner and social creature who has learned from previous Octobers, is the month you prepare for in August and arrive into with the satisfaction of someone who has done the work.

Here is where India's most considered crowd is going next month.

**Where They Are Travelling & Celebrating**



![](/assets/images/posts/img_8118.jpg)



Nestled amidst the Himalayan foothills with panoramic views of the Malsi Forest, **Hyatt Regency Dehradun Resort and Spa** offers an idyllic monsoon retreat for weekend travellers, luxury seekers, and couples planning destination celebrations. With thoughtfully curated seasonal experiences, wellness offerings, immersive dining, and its picturesque setting, the resort is equally suited for a rejuvenating September escape and as a stunning backdrop for destination weddings.



![](/assets/images/posts/domaine-sula-karnataka.png)



Just a short drive from Bengaluru, **Domaine Sula, Karnataka** offers a refreshing introduction to Karnataka’s emerging wine country and an easy way to make a festive break more memorable. Instead of a traditional gift, treat your family to an experience they can enjoy together, with a guided wine tasting, gourmet food, and a leisurely walk through the vineyards. Surrounded by greenery and away from the bustle of the city, Domaine Sula makes for a thoughtful gift for parents, siblings, or loved ones



![](/assets/images/posts/copy-of-dji_0518_1.jpg)



While Pench is renowned for its incredible wildlife, the monsoon transforms the landscape into a lush green paradise, offering a completely different perspective of the forest. At **Saj in the Forest, Pench**, guests can enjoy a luxury retreat surrounded by verdant landscapes, fresh monsoon air, and the tranquillity of nature before setting off on unforgettable jungle safaris through Pench National Park. Designed for travellers seeking immersive wildlife experiences, the resort combines the thrill of spotting majestic wildlife with warm hospitality, authentic local cuisine, and peaceful forest surroundings. Whether you're a wildlife enthusiast, photographer, or simply looking to escape the city this long weekend, Saj in the Forest promises a memorable monsoon getaway



![](/assets/images/posts/niraamaya-retreats-kumarakom.png)



**Niraamaya Retreats Backwaters & Beyond, Kumarakom:** Set along the serene shores of the iconic Vembanad Lake, Niraamaya Retreats Backwaters & Beyond, Kumarakom is where Kerala's monsoon reveals its most enchanting side. As rain gently blankets the backwaters, the retreat transforms into a tranquil haven surrounded by swaying palms, emerald landscapes, and the soothing rhythm of nature.



![](/assets/images/posts/hotel-facade-jw-marriott-bengaluru-prestige-golfshire-resort-spa.jpg)



**JW Marriott Bengaluru Prestige Golfshire Resort & Spa** offers a refreshing alternative to the usual hill station and beach getaways. Located just 25 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport and a convenient drive from Bengaluru, the resort combines the ease of accessibility with the experience of a destination retreat. Nestled at the foothills of Nandi Hills, the 22-acre luxury resort is home to 301 rooms, suites and villas, expansive outdoor spaces, world-class dining, wellness experiences, and one of India's most sought-after championship golf destinations.



**Where They Are Dining**



![](/assets/images/posts/treasures-by-tiara-cafe-launch.jpg2.jpg)





**Treasures Café by Tiara Dhody** is a natural extension of the Treasures by Tiara universe, bringing together jewellery, design and hospitality under one roof. Built as a warm and cozy space, the café offers guests an immersive experience where they can pause, connect and celebrate life's everyday moments. Whether someone is exploring our jewellery collections, catching up over coffee, enjoying a leisurely brunch or celebrating a special occasion, the idea is to create a destination that feels personal, welcoming and memorable. More than just a café, it's an experiential space where beautiful conversations, thoughtful design and meaningful experiences come together.



![](/assets/images/posts/avocado-ceviche.jpg)

![](/assets/images/posts/tobanjan-chicken-dumpling.jpg)



Inspired by the Japanese philosophy of living in the moment, **Ukiyo at The Ritz-Carlton, Pune** offers a thoughtfully curated dining experience where elegant interiors, an intimate omakase counter, a lively terrace bar, and a menu rooted in Japanese craftsmanship come together to create memorable evenings. Guests can explore a range of dishes spanning ceviches, sushi, sashimi, gyoza, robata grills, and signature mains, complemented by an extensive sake selection and signature cocktails.



![](/assets/images/posts/sweeneymarketplace.jpeg)

![](/assets/images/posts/sweeneymaretplace.jpeg)

Bringing the **farm-to-table** philosophy to life, **Sweeney's Marketplace Cookout** reimagines Sunday dining by transforming part of the restaurant into a bustling mini farmers' market. Instead of ordering from a menu, guests can browse through an assortment of fresh vegetables, herbs and proteins before having their meal cooked just the way they like it.

At the heart of the experience is the Make Your Own Thai Curry, where guests can mix and match ingredients to create a meal that's uniquely theirs. Choose from fresh vegetables including zucchini, broccoli, carrots, baby corn, mushrooms, bell peppers and bean sprouts, pair them with silken tofu, cottage cheese, chicken or prawns, and finish with a choice of sauce Panang, Red or Green Thai curry. 



![](/assets/images/posts/concu1.jpeg)

![](/assets/images/posts/concu2.jpeg)

**Conçu** is a premium all-day café and artisanal patisserie that blends European-inspired craftsmanship with warm hospitality. From handcrafted pastries and specialty coffee to leisurely brunches and comforting meals, Conçu offers a thoughtfully curated dining experience designed around slowing down and connecting.



**The September State of Mind**

What unites the travellers, diners, and celebrants heading into September with this quality of intention is a relationship with anticipation that the most interesting Indian social crowd has always cultivated. The advance reservation. The early plan. The decision to be somewhere specific before the crowd arrives, to eat something seriously before the festive menu takes over, to celebrate before the occasion becomes an obligation.

September belongs to the prepared. The rest of October will confirm what they already knew.
