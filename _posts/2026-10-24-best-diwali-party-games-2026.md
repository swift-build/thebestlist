Diwali has always been about the gathering: the lamps, the mithai, the aunties in their finest silk and, somewhere between the main course and midnight, the cards coming out. This year, with the festival arriving on 8 November, the smartest thing a host can do is plan the entertainment as carefully as the menu.

Below, ten games that suit the season, from timeless classics to newer desi favourites, with a note on who each one is really for.
