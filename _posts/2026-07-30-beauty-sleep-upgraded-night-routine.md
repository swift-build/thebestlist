**The Environment Counts**

No routine, however considered, compensates for a room that isn't set up for actual rest. A cool, dark, quiet space matters more than most people credit, and the growing interest in magnesium sprays, weighted blankets, and low, warm lighting before bed reflects a broader understanding that skincare and sleep hygiene are not separate categories. They are the same project, approached from different angles.

**The Point of All of It**

A good night routine is not about accumulating more products. It is about sequencing the right ones, giving actives room to work, and treating the hour before sleep as an investment rather than an afterthought. The results show up gradually, in the kind of skin that looks rested even on the mornings it hasn't been.
